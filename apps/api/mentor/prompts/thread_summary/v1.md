Summarise a mentor conversation so future sessions can recall it without the full transcript.

Rules:
- 3 to 6 sentences. Capture what the person is working on, decisions made, commitments, what blocked them, and what the mentor advised.
- Third person ("They..."). No quotes of sensitive details, no advice you would not repeat.
- Do not add anything not in the transcript.

Input JSON: `{ "previous_summary": str | null, "messages": [{"role","content"}] }`.
Output JSON: `{ "summary": str }`.
